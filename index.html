08 / Fitness data, translated

Five signals become
five human questions

The report compares me with my own recent normal—usually six trailing weeks—not with another athlete.

01

Load & ramp

Is my longer-term fitness trend rising, holding, or easing?

CTL
Chronic Training Load · rolling ~42-day average; a proxy for longer-term fitness
ATL
Acute Training Load · rolling ~7-day average; a proxy for recent fatigue
TSB / form
Training Stress Balance · CTL minus ATL; positive is fresher, negative means carrying fatigue
CTL ramp
week-over-week CTL change; shows whether fitness is building or fading
02

Volume

How much training did I actually do?

Moving time
minutes actually moving; pauses do not count
Training load
Intervals.icu stress score for one workout; weekly load adds them together
03

Intensity

How hard and concentrated was the work?

Load density
weekly load divided by moving hours; how concentrated the stress was
Pace
median running minutes per mile, with implausible values filtered out
04

Consistency

How often did I show up?

Active days
days with at least one training session
Sessions
number of completed workouts in the week
05

Recovery

Do readiness signals support the training story?

HRV
Heart-rate variability · rising versus your normal usually means better recovered
Resting HR
resting heart rate · falling versus your normal usually means better recovered
Sleep
average nightly sleep duration for the week, when available

How it stays honest

Medians soften one strange week. Missing inputs lower confidence and add a caveat—they never become a guess.

buildingholdingrecovering by designdecline possibleoverreaching cautioninsufficient data

11 / Build with confidence

Make every layer
inspectable

Helpful commands turn an agent from a magic demo into a system you can understand, test, and extend.

Sunday Reportraw data → final message
  1. make dump-week DAY=...see raw weekly aggregates
  2. make facts DAY=...inspect five signals + verdict JSON
  3. make report-rawpreview deterministic Telegram output
  4. make reportcompare optional AI narration
  5. make verifyformat, vet, test, build, glossary
PaperdinkSVG → physical pixels
  1. make preview-p1open the exact SVG in Chrome
  2. /previewsee both pages + simulated 1-bit mode
  3. make curl-bmpverify 400 × 300 bitmap encoding
  4. make typecheckcatch Worker contract mistakes
  5. make flashship only after the browser path is right
Do not debug the whole agent at once. Make the source, facts, render, and delivery independently visible.
